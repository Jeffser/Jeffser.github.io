Popcorn

Stream your Jellyfin library with style

Repository
Modern Interface Popcorn features a modern interface based on LibAdwaita and follows Gnome's Human Interface Guidelines to provide the best possible experience.
Media Pages Popcorn provides pages to organize shows and movies. Showcase your library in a stylish and functional way.
Functional Player Popcorn's video player is full of features like subtitles, gestures, trickplay and intro skipper.
Privacy
  • Telemetry: Popcorn does not have any type of telemetry whatsoever.
  • Library: Your media belongs to you, Popcorn won't collect any information on it.
  • Open Source: Popcorn is licensed under the GPL-3.0 license, it's code can be audited freely.
Supporting Creators Popcorn does not facilitate nor supports the piracy of content, any media shown in screenshots has been ripped from physical releases.

Make sure to support companies and creators involved in the media you enjoy.
